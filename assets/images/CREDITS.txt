IMAGE CREDITS - Old Library Yoga
All photographs are downloaded and served locally from assets/images/.
Nothing is hotlinked. No watermarks, no brand logos, no celebrities.

NOTE ON MINORS: this studio teaches mother-and-baby classes, but no
photograph on this site shows a baby or any other child. Images for the
mother-and-baby pages show adults only. This is deliberate.

The set was also chosen for varied subjects: eight different people across
six different rooms, rather than a single stock shoot.
